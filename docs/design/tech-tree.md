## AI Weighting Assumptions

Deterministic AI scoring order:

1. Reject locked/completed techs
2. Penalize pure future-hook techs (especially no unlock payload)
3. Prefer faction-aligned domains
4. Prefer faction-aligned tags
5. Prefer lower rarity and earlier eras
6. Use cost, explicit AI weight hint, display order, and stable ID as deterministic tie-breakers

Faction alignment intent:

- Scientific/Expansionist: Survey/Sensors/Hyperspace/SectorMapping
- Industrial: Shipyard/Production/Orbital
- Militarist: ShipClass/Weapon/Defense/Invasion/Blockade/Command
- Agrarian: Food/Growth/Housing/Terraforming
- Diplomatic: Diplomacy/Stability/Trade

## Future-Hook Policy

`future_hook = true` means technology is intentionally visible in roadmap but may not activate new mechanics yet.

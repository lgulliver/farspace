# FARSPACE Gameplay Loop (First 30 Turns)

This is the intended player flow for the current alpha, not a rigid script.

## Turns 1–5: Establish control

- Start a new game and orient yourself in Galaxy Overview and Sector Map.
- Identify your home system, initial fleets, and first colony.
- Open Research and pick an initial technology.
- Queue early production in your home colony to avoid idle turns.
- Begin scout dispatches to nearby unexplored systems.

Focus: avoid idle colony output and start map knowledge growth immediately.

## Turns 6–15: Expand information and options

- Keep scouts moving; try to maintain continuous exploration.
- Use System view to inspect promising worlds.
- Start planet surveys with science ships where possible.
- Use Empire Overview to catch idle queues, food pressure, and low stability warnings.
- Keep research active every turn.

Focus: convert exploration into actionable intel and prepare for expansion.

## Turns 16–25: Secure new colonies

- Send/position colonizer fleets toward surveyed habitable targets.
- Colonize when fleet position and planet state allow.
- Open new colonies and assign roles based on immediate needs.
- Keep production queues active on all colonies.
- Continue scouting to avoid stagnation and discover contact/combat risks.

Focus: increase colony count while sustaining economy and logistics.

## Turns 26–30: Stabilize and prepare midgame

- Re-check colony roles, queues, and economy summaries.
- Ensure research progress remains aligned with mobility/economy needs.
- Consolidate fleet positioning for defense and response.
- Save the run (`:save`) before moving into longer sessions.

Focus: transition from initial expansion into a stable multi-colony posture.

## Loop priorities every turn

1. Keep research selected.
2. Keep production queues non-idle.
3. Keep at least one exploration/survey action progressing.
4. Check event log for blockers, deficits, and completed tasks.
5. End turn only after economy + fleet intents are set.

## Current-alpha caveats

- Diplomacy is contact visibility, not full negotiation.
- Combat is auto-resolved and summary-driven.
- There is no final victory screen yet, so treat this loop as sandbox progression.

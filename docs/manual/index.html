Install

Recommended: download pre-built binaries from releases.

  • Releases: github.com/lgulliver/farspace/releases
  • Linux/macOS: download binary, chmod +x, run in terminal.
  • macOS first run: clear quarantine with xattr -d com.apple.quarantine farspace.
  • Windows: run farspace.exe from PowerShell or Command Prompt.

Build from source

git clone https://github.com/lgulliver/farspace.git
cd farspace
cargo run --release -p farspace

Empire Profiles

All playable powers in current roster, with signature strengths and strategic posture.

⚙

Ashveran Compact

Pragmatic industrial coalition

A federation of heavy-industry worlds united by supply-chain treaties.

Reliable infrastructure empire with steady production and logistics.

  • Edge: +1 industry per colony
  • Focus: Industrial build-up, defensive security
  • Doctrine: Industrialist, Isolationist
◎

Luminal Traverse

Restless pathfinders

Explorers driven by an obsession with mapping the unknown.

Fast early exploration with a research-led expansion curve.

  • Edge: +1 science per colony, -10% scout cost
  • Focus: Recon reach, frontier scouting, opportunistic growth
  • Doctrine: Explorer, Merchant, Expansionist
✿

Sylvaran Accord

Patient ecological stewards

A biosphere-first collective that values growth and ecological balance.

Food-rich colonies that favour long-term population growth.

  • Edge: +2 food per colony
  • Focus: Population growth, stable colonies, colony arks
  • Doctrine: Expansionist, Biologist
◈

Thalori Exchange

Opportunistic commercial brokers

A merchant alliance that turned commerce into a form of galactic power.

Credit-rich empire that prefers secure trade and measured growth.

  • Edge: +2 credits per colony
  • Focus: Economic throughput, trade-lane security, controlled expansion
  • Doctrine: Merchant, Expansionist
⚔

Vorath Dominion

Martial frontier hegemony

A martial confederation bound by oaths of mutual defense and conquest.

Pressure-oriented power that turns frontier tension into war readiness.

  • Edge: +1 credits per colony, cheaper troops and combat ships
  • Focus: Border pressure, invasion strength, fast escalation
  • Doctrine: Militarist, Imperial
⟁

Elarith Confluence

Measured technocracy

A technocratic council that views scientific advancement as the highest law.

Pure research specialists that convert safe worlds into laboratories.

  • Edge: +2 science per colony, -10% science ship cost
  • Focus: Science ships, safe-world research, fast tech velocity
  • Doctrine: Technologist, Industrialist, Isolationist
☼

Terran Concord

Optimistic, pluralist, science-forward federation

An open Terran union that treats science, dialogue, and exploration as shared civic duties.

Cooperative explorers that open with better relations, lean into research, and keep colonies stable before committing to war.

  • Edge: +1 science per colony, -1 industry per colony, cheaper scouts and science ships
  • Focus: Diplomacy, scouting, science-led expansion
  • Doctrine: Explorer, Technologist, Merchant
▲

Terran Dominion

Authoritarian, expansionist, order-through-force empire

A hardline Terran hierarchy that secures frontier order through rapid militarisation and coercive expansion.

Militarised colonisers that accept worse first contact, cheaper war logistics, and faster escalation when borders tighten.

  • Edge: +1 industry per colony, cheaper troop logistics, shipyards, and combat ships
  • Focus: Aggressive expansion, military buildup, coercive control
  • Doctrine: Imperial, Militarist, Industrialist
